Privacy Policy

How BitBot handles information when you use the Discord moderation bot.

Terms of ServicePrivacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the BitBot Discord application (“BitBot” or the “Bot”) handles information. BitBot provides moderation tools for Discord servers. The Bot does not maintain general user profiles, but it does store moderation records such as logs, warnings, and ban records.

1. Information BitBot processes

To carry out moderation features and keep moderation history, BitBot may process and store:

  • Discord user IDs for users involved in a moderation action.
  • Discord server IDs and, where needed, channel IDs.
  • The moderation action taken, such as a warning, timeout, kick, or ban.
  • The ID of the moderator or automated system that performed the action.
  • The reason supplied for the action, if one is entered.
  • The date and time of the action and related moderation-log details.

BitBot does not create or store general user profiles. However, Discord IDs and moderation records can relate to identifiable Discord users, so they may constitute personal data under applicable privacy laws.

2. How information is used

Information is used to perform moderation commands, maintain warning and ban histories, display moderation logs to authorized server staff, help server administrators review moderation activity, protect the Bot from abuse, and troubleshoot operational issues.

3. Access to moderation records

Moderation records are intended for use by the relevant server’s authorized moderators and administrators through the Bot’s features. Server owners and administrators are responsible for deciding who has permission to access moderation channels, commands, and records.
